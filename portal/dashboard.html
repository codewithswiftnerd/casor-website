General Meetings & Meeting Board

Paperless meetings. Open a meeting before a General Consultative Meeting. While it's live, each office can put its minutes, vision statement or unit report on the public Meeting Board so everyone in the house reads it on their phone. When the meeting ends, press Close meeting: the documents disappear from the public page but stay in the records here for future administrations.

Meeting Board password

Members type this once on their phone to open the Meeting Board. It isn't case-sensitive. Changing it locks everyone out until they enter the new one, so change it after each meeting if you like.

Site Images

How this works: for any photo below, tap Upload photo, pick the picture from your device, then click Save. It updates on the live site right away. No image? The site just keeps using its current default photo, nothing breaks.

Site Content

How this works: edit any text below and click Save - it updates on the live site right away, no redeploy needed. Leave a field untouched and the site just keeps showing its current default text, nothing breaks. Press Enter for a line break inside a field where that matters (like the event date badges or service times).

Subscribers

How this works: anyone who submits the "Stay Connected" form in the site footer shows up here. Removing someone marks them unsubscribed - it doesn't delete their record, so you can Reactivate them later if needed. This list feeds the Compose Newsletter tab - only active subscribers receive a send.

Compose Newsletter

How this works: fill in the fields below, check the preview looks right, then click Send Newsletter. It goes out to every active subscriber (see the Subscribers tab for the count) through a secure server function - your browser never sees the subscriber list or any email password. This can take a little while for a large list; a status message will show when it's done. Sending requires Brevo to be set up first - see SETUP-GUIDE.md if the Send button reports an error.
