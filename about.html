About CASOR

Christ Ambassadors Students' Outreach: who we are, what we believe, and the vision that drives everything we do on campus.

OUR STORY

Raising Kingdom Ambassadors

CASOR UNIPORT is a fellowship of students at the University of Port Harcourt committed to knowing Christ and making Him known across campus. What began as a small gathering of believers has grown into a family that welcomes every student, fresher or final year, into a place to belong.

Every semester, we run Bible studies, prayer meetings, outreach programs and leadership training designed to help students grow in faith while excelling academically, because following Christ was never meant to be separate from everyday student life.

WHAT DRIVES US

Mission. Vision. Values.

Our Vision

To raise a generation of people committed to God and their academics.

Our Mission

To raise a mission-minded generation for effective evangelization of our campus and beyond.

Our Values

Worship, discipleship and fellowship, lived out individually and collectively, with a passion for outreach.

HOW WE SEE IT

The CASOR Cap

Two things we hold together, not one at the expense of the other.

Spiritual Cap

We keep ourselves sanctified and separate, knowing that God has a purpose for every CASORite that only full commitment to Him can fulfil.

Academic Cap

We're just as determined to raise a people committed to God and their academics, so we study seriously as students, not just as believers.

OUR AIMS

What We're Working Toward

  • To live a life of worship and praise, individually and collectively
  • To disciple members to maturity in the course of Christ
  • To build up membership in the body of Christ
  • To serve God and one another in ministry
  • To create a forum to promote love, unity and fellowship among members
  • To involve itself in works of service to God and humanity
